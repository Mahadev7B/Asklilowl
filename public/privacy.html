AskLilOwl Privacy Notice

Effective date: September 14, 2026

AskLilOwl receives the lesson fields and optional ChatGPT-managed image references needed to render the lesson requested in your ChatGPT conversation.

Data use and retention

The AskLilOwl application does not create user accounts, run analytics, sell data, or store lesson content or generated audio in an application database. Lesson data is processed in memory to return the requested interactive view. The hosting provider may process and retain limited operational information, such as IP addresses, request headers, timestamps, and error logs, for security and reliability under its own policies.

Voice narration

After you choose Start lesson, AskLilOwl sends the visible narration text for the current slide to OpenAI to generate speech. Images and quiz answers are not sent for this purpose. The AI-generated audio is streamed back to your lesson and cached temporarily in memory for reliable replay.

Third parties

ChatGPT, OpenAI's Speech API, and the hosting provider process data under their respective terms and privacy policies. AskLilOwl does not send lesson content to a separate language model, image provider, or advertising service.

Your choices

Voice playback is optional. Do not include sensitive personal information in lesson requests. You can stop using or disconnect AskLilOwl through ChatGPT at any time.

Questions can be submitted through the support page.
