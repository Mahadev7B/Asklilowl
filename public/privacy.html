AskLilOwl Privacy Notice

Effective date: September 13, 2026

AskLilOwl receives the lesson fields and optional ChatGPT-managed image references needed to render the lesson requested in your ChatGPT conversation.

Data use and retention

The AskLilOwl application does not create user accounts, run analytics, sell data, or store lesson content in an application database. Lesson data is processed in memory to return the requested interactive view. The hosting provider may process and retain limited operational information, such as IP addresses, request headers, timestamps, and error logs, for security and reliability under its own policies.

Third parties

AskLilOwl does not send lesson content to a separate language model, image provider, advertising service, or text-to-speech provider. ChatGPT and the hosting provider process data under their respective terms and privacy policies.

Your choices

Do not include sensitive personal information in lesson requests. You can stop using or disconnect AskLilOwl through ChatGPT at any time.

Questions can be submitted through the support page.
