AskLilOwl

AskLilOwl turns a completed lesson from ChatGPT into an interactive slide-and-quiz experience.

How it works

The active ChatGPT conversation researches when needed, writes the lesson, and may create educational images. AskLilOwl validates that result and renders it. AskLilOwl does not choose the ChatGPT model and does not call another AI provider.

The MCP endpoint is available at /mcp. See the privacy notice, terms, or support page.
